# Client Business Intelligence & Decision Support Platform

## 1. Business Context

NovaRetail is a retail organization operating across multiple regions and customer segments. The company generates a large volume of transaction data but needs a centralized analytical solution to understand sales performance, profitability, customer behavior and regional trends.

## 2. Business Problem

Management currently lacks a centralized solution for analyzing transaction data and identifying the key factors affecting revenue and profitability.

## 3. Business Objective

Build a business intelligence and decision-support platform that converts raw transaction data into reliable KPIs, analytical insights and actionable management recommendations.

## 4. Key Business Questions

1. What is the overall revenue and profit performance?
2. Which regions generate the highest revenue and profit?
3. Which products and categories contribute most to business performance?
4. Which products or segments generate losses?
5. Which customers contribute the highest revenue?
6. How do discounts affect profitability?
7. How does business performance change over time?
8. Which business areas require management attention?

## 5. Functional Requirements

The solution should:

- Ingest and validate transaction data.
- Clean and transform business data using Python.
- Store structured data in an RDBMS.
- Support analytical SQL queries.
- Calculate business KPIs.
- Provide interactive Power BI dashboards.
- Allow users to analyze performance by time, region, category, product and customer.
- Provide insights that support business decision-making.

## 6. Expected KPIs

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Total Quantity
- Average Order Value
- Profit Margin
- Sales Growth
- Profit Growth
- Regional Performance
- Category Performance
- Customer Contribution

## 7. Expected Business Outcome

The final solution should help management identify performance trends, profitable and loss-making areas, customer and regional opportunities, and potential profitability issues related to discounts.
